THE PERSON BEHIND THE POSTS

Hello, I'm Sasank Manam!

Welcome to my personal blog! I'm Sasank, and I created this website as a place to share my experiences, things I'm learning, travel ideas, and everyday moments that are worth remembering.

As a student, I'm always learning new things and looking for ways to build my skills. This blog is also part of my web development learning journey. It gives me a chance to practice creating a website and bringing together content, design, and interactive features.

What you'll find here

  • My learning experiences and progress with web development.
  • Travel ideas, places I'd like to explore, and memorable experiences.
  • Everyday thoughts, interests, and things I discover along the way.

How I built this website

  • HTML gives each page its structure.
  • CSS controls the colors, layout, and responsive design.
  • JavaScript adds interactive features, including the blog post form.
  • Chart.js displays sample blog analytics.

This website is a class project, and the posts and analytics are examples. I'll continue personalizing the content as I add my own stories.
